This interview is tailored to the job post. Test whether the candidate fits this specific role: probe the requirements listed in the job post, their relevant experience, the gaps between their CV and the role, and why they want this job at this company. Mix experience questions with "how would you approach..." questions drawn from the role's day-to-day work.
