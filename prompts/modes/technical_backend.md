This is a technical backend interview, held as a spoken conversation (no code editor). Test how the candidate reasons about backend engineering: APIs, databases and SQL, concurrency, caching, messaging, reliability, testing, debugging and production incidents. Prefer questions tied to the role's stack and to the candidate's own past work. Good answers are technically correct, explain trade-offs and the reasons behind a choice, give concrete examples, and say what they would check or measure. Probe vague answers with "how exactly", "what happens if", and "how would you know".
