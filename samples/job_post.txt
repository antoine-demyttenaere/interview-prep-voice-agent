Senior Backend Engineer - Northwind Logistics (Lyon, hybrid)

About us
Northwind Logistics runs route planning and real-time tracking for 400 regional delivery companies across France and Spain. Our platform handles 2 million parcel events a day. We are 60 people, 18 in engineering, and we just raised a Series B to expand into Portugal and Italy.

The role
You will join the Tracking team (5 engineers) and own services that ingest carrier events, keep parcel status consistent, and expose it to customers through our public API.

What you will do
- Design and run Python services (FastAPI) on AWS (ECS, RDS Postgres, SQS)
- Improve reliability: our tracking API has a 99.9% SLO and we missed it twice last quarter
- Lead the design of our event pipeline as volume triples over the next 18 months
- Mentor two mid-level engineers and take part in on-call (one week in six)
- Work with product and customer success to prioritise integrations with new carriers

What we are looking for
- 5+ years building backend services in production, Python preferred
- Solid PostgreSQL: schema design, indexing, query tuning
- Experience with message queues and event-driven systems
- You have owned incidents end to end and written postmortems
- Clear communication in English; French or Spanish is a plus

Process
1. 30-minute call with Camille Martin (Talent Acquisition)
2. 60-minute behavioral and experience interview with Julien Roux (Engineering Manager, Tracking)
3. System design interview
4. Meet the team
